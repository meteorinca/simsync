Reference

Use Setup for calibration and Tune for step tests.

PID gains

Kp: response
Raises output in proportion to position error. Too much causes overshoot or oscillation.
Ki: remaining error
Corrects persistent error. Start at zero; add only after proportional and derivative control are stable.
Kd: damping
Slows rapid changes. Increase in small increments when steps overshoot.

Compare steps with the same size and direction. Check both directions under load.

Filters and timing

Feedback LPF smooths feedback. A lower cutoff adds delay. Zero disables it.

Derivative HPF affects damping, not position holding. Start at zero and retest after changes.

Ks controls derivative sampling. Fpid and PWM timers are shared controller settings. M1 and M2 share a PWM timer.

Pause motors and disable jog before applying filters or timing.

Travel limits

Cutoff disables output at the safety edge. Clip constrains commanded targets. Margins apply at both ends of 0 to 1023 counts.

Host bounds must stay at least four counts inside clipping. Center must be inside host bounds.

Display degrees are estimates. Commands, travel limits, and CSV use counts.

Troubleshooting

Feedback moves backward
Stop, cut motor power, and correct wiring or feedback direction before enabling PID.
Motor chatters at rest
Check feedback noise and mechanical play. Try a small deadzone before stronger filtering.
PWM reaches its cap
Lower Kp, or raise PWM max in increments of 5 to 10. Check current and temperature.
Step does not settle
Check travel, feedback direction, friction, and the scope before changing gains.
Controller does not connect
Check the port, firmware, and cable. Close other programs using the serial port.
